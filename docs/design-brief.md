# Design brief

Garrett's decisions, in his words where possible. The model and every building follow this. Dates are when he decided.

## Whole property (2026-11-09)

- Every overhead door is 16 ft tall, except the main house garage.
- Plan for at least 25 of every machine type, everything stored indoors. Realistic contractor head count; only a few crew carriers; equipment can be contracted out.
- 700-acre working forest is a target, not a hard limit.
- Real parcel import and exterior renderings come later.

## Main house (2026-11-09, after a walkthrough of layout v2)

v2 felt "boxy and un-house-like." What bothered him: every room a rectangle, flat same-height ceilings, plain surfaces, few small windows.

- Look: mountain modern. Big glass, heavy timber, stone bases, metal roofs, a mix of gables and low-slope roofs.
- Materials: dry-stack stone base, rough-sawn timber siding, dark standing-seam roof, black windows.
- Massing: a courtyard U with the side wings splayed out 15 to 30 degrees so the courtyard opens to the view.
- Wings: living (great room, kitchen, dining) in the center bar facing the courtyard; primary suite in one wing; kids and guests in the other.
- Ceilings: vaulted great room with timber trusses; vaulted or beamed kitchen and dining; vaulted primary suite with beams; tall entry with a timber canopy; a lower, cozier hearth room for contrast.
- Glass: generous but balanced. Large windows everywhere, full glass walls in the great room and primary suite.
- Nooks: window seats and bays (hearth room, primary sitting, kids' rooms, library), a breakfast nook off the kitchen, kids' bunk and reading nooks, a bar and wine alcove.
- Outdoors: covered courtyard porch with an outdoor kitchen and fireplace, upper decks off the primary suite and kids' floor, a screened porch, a hot tub terrace.
- Fireplaces: great room (floor-to-ceiling stone), primary suite, outdoor.
- Interior detail: timber trusses and beams, stone fireplaces, built-ins, real-looking furniture.
- 3D walkthrough: as real as possible (detailed models, materials, lighting).
- Still applies from before: open and homey, no long hallways, cozy spots, about two-thirds the size of v1; the walkout is excavated south.

## Guest house

- Same family as the main house, smaller: mountain modern, stone, timber, dark metal, vaulted great room with trusses, bays and window seats, covered porch. Big gatherings, sleeps 14, bunk room stays, covered north porch.

## Shops and barns

- Clean metal with timber accents: dark metal, timber entry canopies, stone at the people doors.

## Office and dispatch

- One story, lodge-style public face, at the compound entrance, about 20 to 40 people.
- Reception, 16-seat conference room, 50-seat training room split by an operable wall, full EOC (dispatch for 8 beside a 40-seat operations room), break room, radio and server room, records and plans, showers and lockers, mail and print.
- Garrett's executive office: private bath, private conference, lounge and bar, private entrance. "Seems fine" as designed.

## Car collection building

- Glass showroom character, like a modern gallery, with timber and stone.
- At least 50 cars: 20 on display, 35 in storage, plus motorcycles, UTVs, snowmobiles. Sports and exotics, classics and muscle, trucks and SUVs.
- Lounge with bar, mezzanine overlook, event space, restrooms and kitchenette. Detail bay, wash bay, mechanic area with 4 lifts, body and prep bay, paint booth, tire and alignment, parts and tools. Full climate control.
- Near the entertainment shop, away from the house.

## Entertainment shop

- Match the house: mountain modern.
- Indoor kart track (about 35,000 sq ft is fine as built) connected to the outdoor track, trampoline park with foam pit, 4-lane bowling, arcade, sports court, golf simulators, climbing wall, laser tag, gaming lounge, viewing mezzanine, snack bar, party room, and a commercial kitchen.
- Used by family and guests, plus staff events; staff come escorted through the estate gate. Event parking as planned.

## Compound lodging

- Lodges: all private rooms ("it cuts down on drama"), most for one person, some for couples, each with its own bath.
- Dining and commons: dining hall for 150 with a commercial kitchen, laundry, lounge and game room, fitness and a small store.
- 6 cabins and 8 RV sites, as planned.

## Other compound buildings, to design in detail

- Classroom: three classrooms of 30, a fire behavior and forestry lab, a gear room, about 7,000 sq ft.
- Hangars and aviation: 4 to 6 helicopters and 1 to 2 planes, with maintenance, a pilot ready room, parts, and fuel.
- Pool house: changing rooms, outdoor kitchen, bar, and the outdoor pool.
- Fuel island: canopy, tanks, and storage, laid out with separations.
